walking through a subway, normalized and noise-reduced in Audacity.

Project derivatives: assets/audio/footsteps/step-01.wav through step-06.wav.
Processing: remove sub-bass/DC, soften high frequencies, resample to mono
22050 Hz 16-bit PCM, fade clip edges, normalize, add a brief silent tail.
No synthetic impact layer was added. In-game gait volume and slight pitch
variation are applied to these recordings. tools/prepare_footsteps.py
reproduces the conversion; assets/audio/footsteps/manifest.json includes
the SHA-256 hashes for every original and derived file.
